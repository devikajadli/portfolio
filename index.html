About

Hi, I’m Devika!

Welcome to my little corner of the internet which is a space for writing, projects, art, and everything I’m learning along the way.

By day, I’m a Software Developer with over five years of experience building and supporting enterprise and financial-services applications (working mostly with C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, REST APIs, and SQL/Oracle databases). I love diving into complex systems, solving tricky technical problems, and making business-critical software run smoothly.

I recently moved to Germany to live with my husband, where you can usually find us watching light-hearted comedy shows or exploring new places. When I’m not at my desk, I enjoy cooking, cycling, working out, reading, drawing, and making reels.

Starting this site took a lot of thought, and plenty of self-doubt, and it’s very much a work in progress.

Still, I believe there is so much joy in creating rather than just consuming.

I hope this space inspires you to carve out a creative corner of your own.

Take a look around, and enjoy!
